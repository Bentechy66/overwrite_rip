XP balance curve, by lifetime totals

Each raid's XP depends on how many times you've completed it versus your average completion count on the other four — not on recent streaks. Balanced play stays at full XP forever; lopsided play drifts down smoothly.

The formula

diff = max(0, completionsi − avg(completions of other 4)) how far this raid's total is ahead of your average on the rest multiplier = floor + (1 − floor) × D / (D + diff) D = extra completions needed to roughly halve the bonus above floor

Completions per raid

Why alternating two raids doesn't beat the system: click "Alternate two raids" — both farmed raids land in the mid-50%s, not 100%. The other three you're ignoring keep pulling their own average down, and yours up, relative to them. Only "Spread across all five" holds every raid at 100%, since it makes diff = 0 everywhere.
Tuning: lower D makes the penalty bite sooner (fewer extra completions needed to feel it); raising the floor keeps heavy grinding of one raid worth something for loot even once XP has bottomed out. Base XP per completion is set to 1450 in this demo — swap in your real number, the curve's shape is what matters.
